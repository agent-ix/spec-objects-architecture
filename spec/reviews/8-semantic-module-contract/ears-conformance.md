# EARS conformance review

## Summary

The 113 `SHALL`-bearing statements of issue #8 (StR-001 Stakeholder Need;
FR-001 through FR-006 Description, Behavior and Constraint cells; NFR-001
Statement), the 54 acceptance-criterion rows, and the ten unmodalled rows of
the FR-004 per-type table were read for EARS pattern, single `shall`, named
subject and concrete, verifiable response. Quire 0.31.0 (engine 0.46.0)
reports 19/20 documents grammar-clean with one `[ears:*]` finding
(`vague-response` on StR-001), unchanged under `--strict`; every stated
unwanted-behaviour obligation uses `If … then … SHALL`, and no statement uses
an `On`/`Upon`/`After`/`During` trigger. The dominant defect is semantic
rather than grammatical: one contradiction that makes an acceptance criterion
unsatisfiable by construction (skeleton `title` uniqueness), eight obligations
whose statement, verification, or referenced revision do not agree with each
other, and a family of `Where …` state clauses, passive subjects and
external-system obligations the grammar does not flag. US-001 and the two IT
documents were read for cross-reference consistency only; story and
success-criterion prose are out of EARS scope.

## Verdict

Grammar: pass with one engine finding (StR-001 `be able to`). Semantics: fail
on one high (FND-001) that makes FR-005-AC-8/TC-059 unsatisfiable as written
and needs a wording decision before FR-005 is implementable; the eight mediums
either leave an obligation unverified or leave two statements of the spec
disagreeing; the lows are wording and subject choice.

## Findings

| FND-001 | high | FR-005 Behavior contains two statements that cannot both hold. `Each alternate skeleton SHALL declare the same fields as its table skeleton, under that skeleton's frontmatter id and title; the module therefore ships two files under one id by intent` gives the three alternates (`data_schema.sysml.md`, `queue.sysml.md`, `ui_component.sysml.md`) the same `title` as their table skeletons, while `Every skeleton title SHALL be an Identifier …, distinct across all skeletons` requires all thirteen files to differ. Whether the v1.0.0 schema admits the `semantic` block FR-003 requires is therefore unstated, and FR-001-AC-1 (`Manifest validates against FR-035 JSON Schema`) does not say which copy it validates against; TC-001 resolves it to "the vendored schema" only in the matrix. | Check | Result |
|---|---|
| Explicit subject | Fail: StR-001 obliges non-actors (FND-002), FR-001 uses `the system` and a nominalised event (FND-009), four statements are passive or oblige an artifact (FND-011) |
